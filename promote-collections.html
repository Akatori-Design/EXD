Licenses are needed and royalties are owed for musical and the spoken word compositions anytime, anywhere in the world they are played or used.

Music and the Spoken Word Compositions Royalties include :

  • Played on Pandora, Sirius XM, or other digital or AM/FM radio
  • Streamed on Spotify, Apple, Amazon, YouTube Music, or other streaming services
  • Streamed on video services such as YouTube, TikTok, or other video sites
  • Played in a restaurant, bar, gym, or elevator
  • Played at a live gig
  • Appear in a tv show, film, video game, or app
  • Put onto a CD, vinyl, cassette, or other physical format
  • Has the lyrics available to read on a website or in print
  • Is turned into sheet music or guitar tabs
  • Is covered and released by another artist
