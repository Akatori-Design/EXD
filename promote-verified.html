What's a "Verified Artist"?

A "verified artist" on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music gains special features that enhance their presence and engagement with their audience. This status not only boosts their credibility but also provides them with tools to better connect with their fans and manage their music career.

  • Profile Customization: Add a professional picture, bio, social media links, and featured content.
  • Audience Engagement: Access listener analytics, send fan updates, and share exclusive content.
  • Enhanced Credibility: Verification badge boosts authenticity and discoverability.

Why is it important?

In the rapidly evolving music industry, independent artists often struggle to gain visibility and credibility. Various platforms addresses this challenge head-on by offering a verification status that amps up artists' credibility, ensuring fans know it's really them rocking the charts.

With verification, artists can more effectively attract new fans and unlock lucrative opportunities previously reserved for those signed with big labels.

By providing this level of recognition, verification empowers artists to elevate their careers, ensuring they get paid and gain access to collaborations with iconic brands, thereby democratizing success in the music industry.

What do I need in order to opt-in?

Depending on the platform, several requirements must be met for verification, including proving their own identity or affiliation with the artist or band, and having at least one official music release on the platform that has been delivered and distributed by a Music Distributor or Label.
